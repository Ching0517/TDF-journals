# Week 10: Laser Cutter — Elephant Ring

## 1. Design Concept
The goal this week was to experiment with laser-cut wearable design. I designed a ring where the finger and the ring body come together to form an **elephant head**—with the finger acting as the elephant's trunk and the acrylic/wood frame forming the head, ears, and tusks.

- **Color Coding:**
  - **Red lines:** Vector Cut (cutting through the material)
  - **Blue lines:** Vector Engrave / Score (marking the details)

---

## 2. Prototyping & Generative Workflow

### Ideation: Canva ➔ Claude
Instead of drafting from scratch in CAD, I combined quick layout tools with generative AI:
1. **Canva Mockup:** Created a rough, low-fidelity concept sketch to establish the proportions.
2. **Claude Iteration:** Prompted Claude with the mockup and vector constraints to generate a cleaner, more stylized graphic suitable for laser cutting.

## 3. Sizing & Tolerance (Ring Gauge)

To ensure a comfortable fit, I tested sizing before finalizing the cut:
1. Used a classmate’s pre-cut sizing gauge (a test board with various circle diameters) to find my baseline finger radius.
2. Cut multiple test rings with stepped variations (slightly larger and smaller increments) to account for kerf and joint comfort.

## 5. Reflections
- **Prompting for laser cutting:** Converting AI-generated art into cuttable vector silhouettes requires paying attention to continuous line weight so pieces don't detach unintentionally.
- **Fit & Kerf:** Making slight variations around the measured finger radius was essential—acrylic doesn't flex, so even a 0.2 mm difference affects whether it slides past the knuckle comfortably.
