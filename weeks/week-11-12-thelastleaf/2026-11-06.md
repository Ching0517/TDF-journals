# Week 11 · Nov 6 — Brief, references, and the first mechanism idea

[← The Last Leaf index](README.md) · [Next →](2026-11-07.md)

**Goal:** turn the story of *The Last Leaf* into one interaction that uses the required servo and
ultrasonic sensor.

## What I did

- Wrote down the interaction: the leaf breathes while idle → a visitor approaches (ultrasonic) →
  the leaf drops → the visitor paints a leaf in the air (computer-vision hand tracking in p5.js)
  → the painted percentage lifts the real leaf back → idle again.
- Collected kinetic references for the idle motion: *Kinetic Rain* (many objects on lines moving
  as one wave) and Reuben Margolin's wave sculptures.
- First plan: many leaves on fishing lines, all swaying from **one** mechanism of eccentric
  discs (cams), and all dropping together when someone comes close.
- Made a small table comparing mechanisms (cams, cranks, pulleys) and learned the keywords to
  search for: cam follower, wave machine, eccentric disc.

## Decisions and why

- **One motor for everything.** The brief only allows a servo, so every motion — breathing, drop,
  return — has to come from one actuator. This constraint drives every later decision.

## Problems and open questions

- How do I *release* the lines for a drop if the same mechanism is also holding them up?
